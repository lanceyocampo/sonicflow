LOCAL BY DESIGN

Add music without
giving it away.

Choose audio you own or have permission to use. Playback and storage happen entirely in this browser; SonicFlow has no server and uploads nothing.

  1. 01
    Find properly licensed files

    Use your own recordings, purchased downloads where local playback is permitted, or CC0 / Creative Commons audio that allows your intended use. Keep attribution where required.

  2. 02
    Choose one or more files

    MP3, WAV, OGG, M4A, and FLAC support depends on your browser. Filenames become track titles; you can play, queue, and favorite them like built-in loops.

  3. 03
    Come back on this device

    Imported files are saved in IndexedDB when storage is available. Browser privacy settings or clearing site data can remove them; the original files on your device are never changed.

Adding files to the static project?

Place licensed files in assets/audio/ and artwork in assets/images/, then add matching relative paths and metadata to the track data in script.js. The site needs no build step.

Local file storage is available in this browser.
